## Governance disposition and prevention

Human A0's 2026-09-17 disposition retains the technical evidence and the
Reviewer governance hold, forbids another RealF5 trial, and defers Runtime
Restore. It is a post-execution disposition and is not retroactive
preauthorization. The original pre-execution Human authority receipt was not
independently verified; this does not claim that Human authorization never
existed.

Future material dispatch requires a current `CURRENT.md`, an immutable locator
to the original Human receipt, proof that the receipt predates dispatch and
matches scope, baseline and trial budget, and independent resolvability by the
Builder and Reviewer. A self-declared authority label alone is insufficient;
stale active authority state is `ENTRY_HOLD`.

F-02 remains open because the successful RealF5 path emitted both
`REAL_F5_DISPATCH=PASS` and `F5_EXECUTED=NO`. Durable lifecycle, execution
context, P0-P5 receipts, barrier journal and final physical state establish
that this trial executed. No machine parser was found, but the operator-facing
contradiction requires a separate TDD reporting correction. A RealF5 rerun for
log cosmetics is forbidden.
